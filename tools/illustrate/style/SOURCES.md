# Style plates

Crops of woodblock prints by Ohara Koson (1877-1945), scanned by the
Rijksmuseum Amsterdam and released CC0 (public domain). Each is cropped
to the birds. `illustrate.py` sends the first three images here for birds,
and uses only their printing technique.
